The game

What it is

You make a character, hang around Treehouse Village, head out into the field, fight, gather stuff, and come back to town. There’s a party system, offline progress, and class mentors once you hit level 10. It’s meant to feel like those older side-scrolling online RPGs a lot of us grew up on — not a remake of any of them.

In game right now

  • A town and 6 regions, each with its own boss
  • Real-time combat, with bosses that get angrier
  • Four classes with job changes at levels 10, 30 and 70
  • A hero that walks, blinks and wears what you put on
  • Gathering with tools, crafting, a shop and a world map
  • Quests, a monster book, gathering while you’re away
  • Party play online, now with passwords

Still messy

  • Many monsters and items have no art yet
  • Only about a hundred quests so far, the story ones are next
  • Few sounds, and some areas still look bare
  • Ledges and traps still use stand-in art
  • No public build download yet

Engine

Made with Godot 4.7. You can play solo, and parties online work in testing, with passwords and a protected connection. A public server is not open yet.

Updates

What’s been going on

Recent work, in normal language.

Levelling

Levelling the classic way

Levelling is now longer and steadier, like the older side-scrollers: each level takes real hunting, and quests give a solid push. Parties split one pot of experience by level and damage, plus a bonus for grouping, so nobody gets a free ride. Falling in battle costs a little less than before, and a Warding Charm from the shop saves you from the loss once. The third job change now comes at level 70.

Your hero

A hero that feels alive

Your character is now built from layers, so the clothes you wear show on them. They have a new walk, they blink, and they sway a little while standing. Other players online now show up as their real characters too.

Sign-in

Passwords and a safe connection

Online play now asks for a password. The first time you log in with a name, it becomes yours. The connection between the game and the server is protected, and the server stores accounts locked away, so the files on their own are useless to anyone who gets hold of them.

Gathering

Tools and fair prices

Gathering needs a tool now: pickaxes, axes, fishing rods and sickles in three tiers. Better tools open harder spots and work faster. Shop prices were rebalanced so selling crafted goods can’t beat going out and fighting.

World

Traps, ledges and fair bosses

Some areas now have thorns, sharp coral and bog pools to jump over, plus low ledges to hop up on. Bosses can be cleared a few times a day, and their big attacks now have sounds. Picking up loot works one item at a time, the way it should.

Menus

New windows

The bag and equipment screens were rebuilt, quests are grouped by region, the world map shows every area, and the character creation screen got a fresh look.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is this MapleStory / IdleOn / Fiesta?

No. Those are influences. Different game, different world, different art.

Can I download and play?

Not publicly yet. Builds are still changing too often. When there’s something worth handing out, it’ll show up here.

Why “MMORPG” in the name?

It’s about the kind of game it is — classes, town, grinding, goals. It does not mean full MMO servers are coming next week.

Who’s making this?

Solo / small-scale project. This site is the public progress log.
