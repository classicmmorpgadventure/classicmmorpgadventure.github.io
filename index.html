The game

What it is

You make a character, hang around Treehouse Village, head out into the field, fight, gather stuff, and come back to town. There’s a party system, offline progress, and class mentors once you hit level 10. It’s meant to feel like those older side-scrolling online RPGs a lot of us grew up on — not a remake of any of them.

In game right now

  • A town and 6 regions, each with its own boss
  • A first dungeon, The Hollow Hive, on Normal and Hard
  • Real-time combat, with bosses that get angrier
  • Four classes with job changes at levels 10, 30 and 70
  • Gear in 11 slots, with rolled stats and region sets
  • Seven pets that follow you and pick up drops
  • Gathering, crafting, quests, cards and leaderboards
  • Party play online, on a server that keeps everything

Still messy

  • About half the monsters still need their art
  • Only about a hundred quests so far, the story ones are next
  • Few sounds, and some areas still look bare
  • Only the first region has its new ground and ledges so far
  • No public build download yet

Engine

Made with Godot 4.7. You can play solo, and parties online work in testing, with passwords and a protected connection. A public server is not open yet.

Updates

What’s been going on

Recent work, in normal language.

Pets

Seven pets to find

A cat, a dog, a bunny, a fox, a frog, a turtle and an owl. Each one is a rare drop from a region boss or the new dungeon. Your pet follows you around, you can give it a name, and it picks up drops for you. They breathe, blink now and then, and the owl flies along behind you, then lands next to you when you stop. The little ones running around this page are them.

Dungeon

The Hollow Hive

The first dungeon is in Honey Woods. Bring a Honeycomb Key, fight through the hive with your party and face Queen Honeywing at the end. Halfway through she calls her guards and stings faster, and on Hard her guards come pouring out even sooner. If you are lucky she drops a honeycomb throne you can sit on, or a crown you can wear.

Gear

More slots, and sets

Gear now goes in eleven slots, with gloves, a cape, earrings, a ring, a pendant and a shield joining the old ones. Each region has its own set, and wearing two, three or four pieces of it gives you a bonus. You can also wear a look over your gear: it changes how you appear, never your stats.

Records

The Hall of Records

A new sign in Treehouse Village opens the leaderboards: highest level, most achievements, biggest collection, most party quests, and the fastest dungeon and jump quest times. Some boards start fresh every week, so there is always a new race to win.

Art

A big art pass

Almost every item in the game now has its own detailed picture (the newest gear is next), and coins have art too: Bronze, Silver, Gold and Starsteel. There are eighteen silly chairs to sit on, from a rubber duck to a honeycomb throne, many of them moving. Monster cards got frames and little scenes, and Leaf Meadow got new ground, ledges, ropes, ladders, portals and decorations.

Fixes

Smoother behind the scenes

Enemies that seemed to turn invisible now show up where they really are, and a lot of small bugs found while playing the dungeon are gone. The server got easier to run and test, with a developer rank that helps me try things out quickly.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is this MapleStory / IdleOn / Fiesta?

No. Those are influences. Different game, different world, different art.

Can I download and play?

Not publicly yet. Builds are still changing too often. When there’s something worth handing out, it’ll show up here.

Why “MMORPG” in the name?

It’s about the kind of game it is — classes, town, grinding, goals. It does not mean full MMO servers are coming next week.

Do pets make you stronger?

No. Pets have no stats: they follow you, look cute and pick up your drops. Looks never change power in this game.

Who’s making this?

Solo / small-scale project. This site is the public progress log.
